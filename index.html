How this is calculated

Total cost is everything you pay (purchase, fees, insurance, road tax, fuel or electricity, upkeep, battery replacements, lease or tickets) minus what you get back when you sell. Resale values follow a curve where vehicles lose value fastest in the first years. Upkeep rises 5% a year as vehicles age. Public transport fares rise 4% a year.

All assumptions live in data.js. Insurance and resale values are estimates, so check your own quotes.
